How it works

Parents set it up, your child plays. Three taps, three steps to a word.

Record your voice

In the parents' section, record words and add photos of Mom and Dad. It's protected by a parental gate.

Your child taps

The picture grows and says its name in your voice, in your language, then in a second one.

The word comes alive

Second tap: what it does (“The dog barks: woof-woof!”). Third tap: a short cartoon with real sounds.

Questions & answers

What age is ToT for?

Little ones from 12 months. Big pictures, simple phrases and one-tap controls.

Where are recordings and photos stored?

Only on your device. We upload nothing to any server. Deleting the app deletes the recordings too.

Does it need the internet?

No. ToT works offline; a connection is needed only to make a purchase through the App Store.

What does the full version include?

The Animals, Food and Vehicles sets, recording words in your voice, bilingual mode and real photos of Mom and Dad in the book. One purchase, no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

In the parents' section, tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID.

How much does ToT cost?

It's free to download: the First Words set and narration in one language are available right away. The full version is a single one-time in-app purchase, no subscription. You can see the current price on the App Store.
